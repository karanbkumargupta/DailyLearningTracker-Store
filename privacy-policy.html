Privacy Policy

Daily Learning Tracker · Last updated: June 17, 2026

Daily Learning Tracker ("the app") is designed to respect your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. We have no servers, no accounts, and no analytics or advertising trackers.

Where your data is stored

Everything you type — your learning notes and the dates they were created — is stored locally on your own device using your browser/operating system storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Deleting your data

You can delete any individual entry at any time using the delete (×) button next to it. You can also remove all stored data by clearing the app's site data / storage from your browser or by uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
